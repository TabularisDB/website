# Still working on GTA VI day?

GTA VI is on the calendar. So is your 10 a.m. standup.

If your getaway vehicle today is a SQL client, I made **Vice City for Tabularis**. **After Dark** gives your queries neon pink, mint, and deep purple. **Daybreak** swaps midnight for peach and rose. Both palettes cover the app, SQL syntax, and result grid, so even your joins get a little sun.

![After Dark colors the SQL editor and the Vice City Garage result grid in purple, pink, and mint](/img/posts/vice-city-launch-day/after-dark.png)

![Daybreak shows the same SQL query and garage inventory in peach, rose, and green](/img/posts/vice-city-launch-day/daybreak.webp)

## Take a lap while the query runs

Hit **Play** right here, jump the barricades, and grab stars and shields. Stick around long enough and six stars bring backup patrols, a helicopter, and an armored roadblock. Your query can wait another minute.

[Open the game in its own tab](/games/vice-city).

## Get the theme

In Tabularis Web, go to **Settings → Appearance → Browse themes in Plugins**. Find **Vice City for Tabularis** and install it from the registry. Back in **Appearance**, pick **After Dark** or **Daybreak**. Set the SQL editor theme to **Same as App** for matching syntax colors.

The registry package contains the two color palettes. Want the sunset backgrounds and the getaway game inside Tabularis Web? Follow the [optional local setup](https://github.com/TabularisDB/tabularis-vice-city-theme#optional-animated-web-preview). Or just hit **Play** above.
